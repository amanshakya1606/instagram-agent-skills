# Profile optimizer

A prospect who gets a DM or comment checks the profile within seconds. It must answer: who is this, who do they help, what proof, what next.

## Audit (read the profile in the browser, score each 0-2)
1. **Name field** — searchable keyword, not just brand name. e.g. `Your Brand | Marketing Agency [City]`.
2. **Profile photo** — clear logo, readable at 40px, high contrast.
3. **Bio line 1** — who you help + outcome. **Line 2** — how/services. **Line 3** — proof (real number). **Line 4** — CTA with emoji pointer.
4. **Link** — one clear destination (WhatsApp / booking / audit form), not a wall of links.
5. **Category + contact buttons** — business category set, email/WhatsApp button on.
6. **Highlights** — Services, Results, Reviews, Process, About, FAQs; branded covers.
7. **Pinned 3 posts** — (a) who we are/what we do, (b) best case study, (c) offer/how to start.
8. **Grid** — consistent colours/fonts, last 9 posts show face/proof/value mix.
9. **Consistency** — posting gap, dead periods.

## Output
- Score /18 with the 3 highest-impact fixes first.
- 3 bio options (≤150 chars each), Hinglish or English per brand voice, keyword in first line.
- Name-field option, highlight list with titles, pinned-post suggestions (which existing posts, or what to create).

## Applying changes
Profile edits are account-setting changes: show the exact new text, get the user's yes, then edit via Edit profile in the browser and confirm it saved. Log it.
