# Hashtag & SEO

Instagram ranks by what the post is clearly about. Keywords in caption, on-screen text, audio/spoken words and alt text matter more than hashtags.

## Steps
1. From the post topic + ICP, pick 1 primary keyword phrase (what the client would type, e.g. "digital marketing agency in [city]", "restaurant marketing ideas").
2. In the browser, type it in Instagram search and note autocomplete suggestions and the top posts' wording → 3-5 secondary phrases.
3. Place them:
   - Primary phrase in caption line 1 and on screen in the first 3 seconds / slide 1.
   - Secondary phrases naturally in the body.
   - Alt text: plain description containing the primary phrase.
4. Hashtags: 3-5 only — 1 niche-service, 1 industry of the ICP, 1 location, 1-2 topic. Check each tag page: skip banned/irrelevant/spam-filled tags and tags over ~5M posts.
5. Add a location tag when targeting a city.

## Output
A small block for the post: primary keyword, secondary keywords, 3-5 hashtags, alt text, location. Keep a running list in `brand/keywords.md` so sets rotate and are not identical on every post.
