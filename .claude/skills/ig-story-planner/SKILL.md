# Story planner

Stories warm existing followers into conversations; replies to stories open the DM thread legitimately.

## Daily sequence (3-5 frames)
1. **Hook frame** — question or bold statement about a client problem.
2. **Value frame(s)** — quick tip, screenshot of own work, behind the scenes.
3. **Interaction frame** — poll / quiz / question sticker / slider.
4. **CTA frame** — "Reply 'AUDIT' and I'll review your page" (2-3 times a week, not daily).

## Weekly themes
Mon tip · Tue poll about their marketing problem · Wed behind the scenes · Thu client result (real) · Fri myth vs fact · Sat Q&A box · Sun personal/team.

## Output
For each frame: text on screen (≤15 words), sticker type, background (photo/colour/post reshare), and the follow-up DM to send to people who vote or reply (personal, no link in first message). Save in `content/stories/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

Publishing stories from desktop web is limited; prepare 1080×1920 images with `ig-carousel-designer` and let the user post from the phone if the browser cannot. Poll/reply follow-ups go through `ig-dm-inbox` with approval.
