# Competitor analysis

## Pick 5-8 accounts
3 direct competitors (same city/services), 3 larger Indian agencies or marketing creators, 1-2 accounts the ICP loves. Save the list in the brand profile.

## For each (public pages, browser)
- Bio promise, offer, CTA, link destination, highlights.
- Followers, posts/week, format mix (reel/carousel/static).
- Top 5 posts of the last 60 days by views/likes: topic, hook, format, length.
- Weak posts: what flopped.
- Comments: what the audience asks for, complaints, repeated questions (= content ideas).
- How they get leads (DM keyword, link, WhatsApp, lead magnet).

## Synthesis
- **Table**: account × positioning × best format × best topics × lead mechanism.
- **Patterns** that work across accounts.
- **Gaps**: topics/industries/languages nobody covers well (e.g. Hindi animated explainers for local businesses).
- **10 content ideas** and **1 positioning angle** for the brand.

Save to `research/competitors-YYYY-MM.md`. Learn from structure and topics; do not copy captions, designs or reels.
