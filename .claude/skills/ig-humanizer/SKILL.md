# Humanizer

Short texts (comments, DMs) expose AI habits the most. Run this on every draft before the approval batch.

## Remove / rewrite
- **Stock words**: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, crucial, game-changer, unlock, elevate, "in today's fast-paced world", "testament to".
- **Reel/caption clichés**: "stop scrolling", "in today's video", "follow for more", "tag someone who needs this", "double tap if", "the algorithm loves", "run don't walk". Hindi equivalents too: "aaj ki video me", "last tak dekhiye", "like share subscribe".
- **Structures**: "It's not just X, it's Y"; three-item lists by reflex; one-word rhetorical questions ("Result?"); emoji bullet lists; 3+ SHOUTED words; hashtag walls; every sentence the same length.
- **Typography**: em dashes → comma or full stop; curly quotes → straight; "…" → "..."; no invisible characters.
- **Openers**: generic praise ("Great post!", "Bahut badhiya!"), the person's name with an exclamation mark, leading emoji.

## Add
- One concrete detail per text: a number, a name, a place, a thing visible in their post.
- Uneven rhythm: a short line next to a longer one.
- How people actually type Hinglish: "kr", "nhi" are fine in DMs/comments if the brand voice allows; English business words stay English.
- First person and contractions; one point per message.

## Check (score each 0-2, need ≥8/10 and nothing at 0)
1. Varied sentence length 2. Specific detail present 3. No stock phrases 4. Clean typography 5. Sounds like `brand/brand-profile.md` voice

Could this exact text sit under any other post or go to any other person? If yes, rewrite.

## Limits
Do not invent facts to sound specific — use a `{placeholder}` and ask. This is about writing well, not about hiding that AI assists; if someone asks, the brand says so honestly.
