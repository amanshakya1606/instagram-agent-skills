# Hook bank — 26 formulas

Formula set adapted from Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill (MIT). Hindi examples are written for a marketing agency; replace `{…}` with real, true details. Never invent numbers — if a number is unknown, write `{aapka number}` and ask the user.

| # | Formula | Template | Hindi example | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cost Confession | {amount} is what {mistake} cost me | "₹{amount} doob gaye sirf ek galat ad setting se." | number is vague or made up |
| 2 | Negative Command | Stop {X}. Do {Y} instead | "Roz post karna band karo. Ye karo." | viewer doesn't have that habit |
| 3 | Nobody Tells You | Nobody tells you {truth} | "Koi nahi batata ki followers se sale nahi aati." | truth is already common |
| 4 | Replacement | {this} replaced {expensive thing} | "Ye free tool ₹5,000 wale software ka kaam karta hai." | overstated |
| 5 | Time Collapse | used to take {long}, now {short} | "Pehle ek post me 2 ghante, ab 15 minute." | ratio unbelievable |
| 6 | Receipt | I did {thing} for {N days}, real numbers | "30 din roz reel daali. Ye rahe asli numbers." | no proof shown |
| 7 | Wrong Way, Right Way | you're doing {X} wrong, not your fault | "Aap hashtags galat laga rahe ho, aur ye aapki galti nahi hai." | criticism without reassurance |
| 8 | Insider Leak | {N years} inside {thing}, what we never said | "{N} saal agency chalayi. Ye baat client ko koi nahi batata." | secret is generic |
| 9 | The Steal | steal this {artifact} | "Ye caption template chura lo." | artifact not on screen |
| 10 | If This, Then Watch | if you {situation}, this fixes it | "Agar aapki dukaan ka Instagram 500 followers pe atka hai, 20 second ruko." | situation too broad |
| 11 | Numbered + Favourite | {N} things, number {k} nobody uses | "3 cheezein jo leads laati hain. Teesri koi use nahi karta." | list above 7 |
| 12 | The Objection | "{objection}." Fine, here's what works | "'Instagram hamare business ke liye nahi hai.' Theek hai, ye dekho." | objection invented |
| 13 | Before/After | old → new → the one lever | "Pehle: 200 views. Baad: 20,000. Sirf pehli line badli." | many changes named |
| 14 | Callout | {specific group}, you're ignoring this | "Salon owners, ye ek cheez aap miss kar rahe ho." | group too broad |
| 15 | Flop Record | first {N} failed, what changed | "Pehli 12 reels flop. 13vi me ye badla." | success looks like luck |
| 16 | Verbatim Question | "{question as asked}" | "'Bhaiya ads ka kitna lagega?' Har hafte ye sawaal aata hai." | question polished too much |
| 17 | Head to Head | {A} vs {B}, ran both | "Reels vs carousel. Dono 30 din chalaye." | no winner picked |
| 18 | Cold Open Demo | start mid-action | (screen pe seedha result) "Dekho ye kya karta hai." | payoff too slow |
| 19 | Deadline | {thing} changes on {date} | "Diwali se pehle ye set kar lo." | fake deadline |
| 20 | Permission | you don't need {thing} | "Aapko mehenga camera nahi chahiye." | nothing offered instead |
| 21 | Mid-Sentence Start | "...and that's when {turn}" | "...aur tab client ne bola, 'band kar do sab.'" | setup never explained |
| 22 | Contrarian Flip | known saying, inverted | "Content is king? Nahi. Distribution." | saying nobody uses |
| 23 | Statistic | {N}% of {group} {fact} | "{N}% log khareedne se pehle Instagram check karte hain." (source needed) | unsourced |
| 24 | Reveal | this is the new {thing} | "Ye hai Instagram ka naya feature." | doesn't say what it changes |
| 25 | Someone Else's Result | one {person} got {outcome} | "Ek cafe ne 30 din me walk-ins double kiye." (real, named with permission) | reads like rumour |
| 26 | Superlative | the {fastest/only} {thing} is {answer} | "Local business ka sabse sasta marketing: Google profile." | soft answer |

## Scoring a hook (pick the best of 3)
Rate each 0-2: **Specific** (number, name, concrete noun) · **Front-loaded** (key word in first 4 words) · **Addressed** (speaks to one kind of viewer) · **Stakes** (something to gain/lose) · **Speakable** (≤3 seconds aloud, ~8 words). Specificity matters most — when two tie, the more specific wins. A score is a filter for weak hooks, not a prediction of virality.

## Beat checks for reel scripts
Hook ≤3 s · no beat longer than 4 s without a visual change · no two lines in a row without something concrete · ending loops back to the opening · Hindi voiceover pace ≈ 150-165 words/min.
